Junk Removal vs a Dumpster vs Doing It Yourself

People ask me whether to hire Trash Titans, rent a dumpster, or load a truck and go. All three can be the right call. I run a junk removal business, so this is written from my side of the trailer, but I will tell you when the other options make sense.

I do not post dollar prices. A price without a photo would be a guess. The same three things that set every quote I give are volume, weight, and how hard the stuff is to reach.

What changes the cost

Volume is how much of the trailer the load fills. A few long boards can take the whole bed. A box of household junk takes a small slice, and a small job can sometimes ride with another stop, which keeps the dump fee from landing on one person.

Weight is what the landfill charges for. They bill by the ton. A refrigerator is about 200 pounds. Tile, concrete, wet wood, and old appliances weigh more than they look. My trailer also has a weight limit, so a very heavy load may not fill the bed.

Access is labor, and labor is time on site. A pile at the curb is the fastest job I do. Stairs, a tight hallway, or a pile at the bottom of a slope takes longer. That shows up in the quote for me, and it shows up as your Saturday if you do it yourself.

Labor, time, permits, and the driveway

Labor I load the household junk, furniture, and appliances. You load it, or you hire someone else to. You load it, drive it, and unload it.
Time I show up, load, and leave. Most jobs are same week. A photo by text is usually enough to start the quote. The box sits while you fill it, for however many days the rental includes. You spend the trip, the line at the scale, and maybe a second trip.
Permits I am not leaving a container behind, so there is no dumpster permit on your job with me. If the only place for the box is the street, your city or HOA may want a permit. I will not guess those rules for you. Check before you order one. You need a vehicle that can legally carry the load.
Driveway The truck and trailer are there for the load, then they are gone. The box needs a place to sit. That can block a driveway, a parking spot, or a tight rural pull-off. You need room to park your own truck while you load.

Who each option is for

Hire me when the load is household junk, furniture, appliances, a garage, an estate, or a commercial cleanout, and you do not want to do the lifting. Also when the stuff is inside and has to come down stairs. Curbside is the simplest version: put it out, and I load it.

A dumpster can make sense on a long remodel, when debris shows up every day and you have a place to park the box. I still haul construction debris when you want the pile gone in one trip instead of living with a container.

Doing it yourself can make sense for a small, light load you can already lift, if you have a truck and the time. It gets old fast once the pieces are couches, mattresses, or appliances.

Storm debris and yard debris are part of what I haul, but they are not the main thing I do. Most of my calls are the junk inside the house and the garage.
